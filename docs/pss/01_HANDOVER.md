# PourSocialScore (PSS): the addon as it works (state at release 3.7.0.114)

**The state document (baseline 02 E11, the author, 2026-10-08):** how the addon works at the last full release. It is re-summarized at every full release from the plan's done stages. 3.7.0.114 (Addon manager, Drop Cheapest Thing, Warehouse and money) added the Addon Manager tab, Enabled components, PSS Drop Cheapest Thing, the Warehouse with money and Search Everywhere, safer upgrades and the service bus underneath them; everything else is as it was at 3.5.0.62. Status is in 06; the author's preferences are in 11.

## 1. Purpose
PourSocialScore is the author's World of Warcraft chat moderation addon (GitHub `racetracks`, in-game author Boaties). Clients: Retail (Midnight, Interface **120007**) and Camelot (Interface **16001**).

What it does:
- **Player Ignore List.** Unlimited and account-wide (players, NPCs, whole servers), with notes, expiry (applied by itself at login, after combat once a day and when a list opens), per-player **W I G P C** flags (whisper, invite, guild, party, chat; ticked = allowed), exceptions to the defaults for listed people, a detail pane, auto-decline of duels/trades/invites, and Player Search via /who.
- **PSS is authoritative over Blizzard's ignore list:** a per-player tick "Also on Blizzard's ignore list" (off by default) puts a listed player on Blizzard's list; a Blizzard Ignore lists the player in PSS (note "Synced from Blizzard ignore list on <date>") and takes them off Blizzard's list; the existing Blizzard list is synced across at login and left in place; removal from either removes from both.
- **Right-click menus:** a "PSS" heading with Block Player and Block Guild (Unblock when listed; no Block Guild without a unit, a guild, or for your own guild).
- **Names with a surname (Camelot):** one normaliser, `M.PSS_NormName(name, realm, unit)`; a unit API's second value is a surname when `UnitRealmRelationship(unit)` is 1, giving `First Last-<own realm>`. Every name caller uses it; saved `First-Last` records are repaired once by upgrade step 10 on Camelot saves.
- **Guild Ignore List.** /who scans split until each query returns under 50, Scan All (keybind), Custom Scan, 6 s cooldown, level cap, Guild Search, Guild Exclusion List.
- **Managed community lists.** Shipped guild lists ("Managed Communities - Asmongold", 119 guilds), switched on by Chat Filters guild rules, off by default. Built with `tools/PSS_BuildManaged.lua`.
- **Chat filter engine.** Built-in filters off by default. Custom rules use a tag language compiled to closures (about 30 us per line). A chat link converter on the Chat Filters tab.
- **Block history and counts.** Counts per session, per 24 h and all time; the block log (2000 lines) plus kept lines per listed person (04). Removing an owner never loads Logging: `History.ClearOwner` drops its waiting lines and marks it in `PourSocialScoreDB.purge` (cap 200), applied when Logging loads.
- **Upgrades:** a step registry (`PSS_Upgrade.lua`, steps 1 to 10, step 10 runs on Forever only (SAV-00093), each feature-tagged and safe to run twice); keys are deleted only from one `RETIRED` list. The first login after any version change prints the upgrade report (a line per feature that changed, "SavedVariables performance cleanup" always, then "Saved data upgraded to X (step N)."); a downgrade prints one line and keeps the newer record.
- **Forward-compatible, sparse saves:** every saved field is in `M.PSS_SCHEMA` (`PSS_Schema.lua`; `check_schema.lua`); unknown fields and newer layouts are kept; rules this version cannot use are kept, not applied, reported and exported by `/pss export unused`; exports are format 2 (`v`, unknown fields in `more`). Records save only what is not derivable (member fields through accessors, counts without zero fields, W I G P C only when false, log `m` only when needed), a guild rule keeps at most 1500 members, the player list is one array of records (`list`, routed to `PSS_PlayersDB`), and custom filters have a never-reused id (`nextFilterId`; history keyed `f:<id>`). On the 3.4.0.28 save `PSS_GuildsDB` went from 460,922 to 227,885 bytes.
- **Graphics settings (`/pss gfx`, Camelot only, 3.5.0.57):** export every graphics setting (the client's Graphics console category, no secure, read-only, locked or `addon*` CVar) as text, import one with a preview of each change, a 15 s keep-or-revert prompt after the reload (Revert, Escape or no answer restores), and the last 5 sets kept for restore or export. Two sparse saved keys (`gfxSets`, `gfxPending`, in `PSS_OptionsDB`); Import and Restore refused in combat. Logic `PSS_Graphics.lua` (loads only where the gfxCommand cap holds) (Libraries; Retail defines nothing), popups in `PSS_Popups.lua`, core's one line starts the keep prompt through `M.PSS_GfxAsk`.
- **Macro Helper (`/pss macro`, Camelot only, 3.5.0.61):** writes the macros of `PSS_MacroData.lua` (with PSS_Macros.lua, loads only where the macroCommand cap holds) (a builder; today `global`: Trinket 1, Trinket 2; `warrior`: Attack), each as an account or a character macro (the macro's store, then its set's, then account for global and character for a class, or one store for the run: `/pss macro <set> create [account|character]`); `CreateMacro` / `EditMacro` only, by name per store, never deleted, refused in combat, class sets only on that class; no saved data and nothing kept after a command. The author's macros are drip-fed after the release (issues #159 to #169).
- **Enabled components (3.6.0.69):** `useAddons` (off), `useLogging` (on), `useGfx` (off, Camelot), `useFiltering` (on), `useDtt` (off), `useWarehouse` (off), `useBags` (declared, hidden until 3.8.0); read once per session through `M.PSS_On`, a change applies after a reload; a part that is off loads and registers nothing.
- **Addon Manager (3.6.0.64 to 3.6.0.91; feature [`addon-helper`](https://github.com/racetracks/Pour-Social-Score_Scratch/tree/main/documents/features/addon-helper)):** the Addon Manager tab (`/pss acp`, `/acp`, binding, Addon Compartment) with a folding page list (`ns.NewNav`): Addons (grouped searchable list, per character or all, Load, Enable/Disable All, Undo, Reload with a change count, protected addons), Addon Profiles the ACP way (Core 1 to 5, Layered 6 to 25, class; side menu and a tick per place), Optional Features, Import/Export, the lists' notes, All Options. Profiles are account wide owned keys (`addonSetsOf` and friends, upgrade step 11 from `PSS_CharDB`). Reload on Camelot through our action-button overlay (02 section 12). No memory or CPU figures.
- **PSS Drop Cheapest Thing (3.6.1.93 to .97; feature [`money-helper`](https://github.com/racetracks/Pour-Social-Score_Scratch/tree/main/documents/features/money-helper)):** minimap button and `/pss dtt` (`/dropcheap`, `/dtct`): destroys or sells the cheapest junk item, one per click, out of combat; Feature Options rows, Always/Never item lists, optional merchant Sell junk button; acts logged as `dtt` lines.
- **Service bus and trigger engine (3.7.0.98 to .104; feature [`service-bus-helper`](https://github.com/racetracks/Pour-Social-Score_Scratch/tree/main/documents/features/service-bus-helper)):** record sets and services (`M.PSS_Provide`, `M.PSS_Call`, `PSS_Bus.lua`), key/value stores and queries (`M.PSS_Store`, `M.PSS_Query`), entity ids and owned keys (`M.PSS_EntityId`, `M.PSS_Owned`), the event-driven engine (`PSS_Watch.lua` core, `PSS_Trig.lua`; `M.PSS_Want`; one one-shot timer), budgets as build gates (`bus_budgets.lua`).
- **Warehouse and money (3.7.0.106 to .112; features [`warehouse-helper`](https://github.com/racetracks/Pour-Social-Score_Scratch/tree/main/documents/features/warehouse-helper), [`money-helper`](https://github.com/racetracks/Pour-Social-Score_Scratch/tree/main/documents/features/money-helper)):** behind `useWarehouse`: `PSS_WarehouseDB` (bags, bank, mail, void, auctions, equipped, guild banks, warband, currencies, gold, keyed by entity id), tooltip holder lines through one gated post-hook, Search Everywhere (`/pss wh`, `/syn`, keyword language), `/pss money`, `/pss wh import` from Syndicator and the thin `Syndicator` shim; `whRealms` this realm or all; orphaned data kept hidden until removed.
- **Safer upgrades (3.7.0.106):** each step part guarded, one undo copy (`/pss upgrade undo`), `/pss status`; chat filter start-up steps guarded and `/pssguild chat` counters (`M.PSS_CoreStats`); secret-order lint `check_secretorder.lua`.
- **One window** (09) in three looks: Modern, Classic and Dark.

Goal: "global" moderation through shared lists. Sharing happens through shipped lists and Import/Export.

## 4. Real names (the project's own objects)
- **Namespace:** everything hangs on `M`, shared through the global `PourSocialScore_NS`. The window's private file table is `ns` (`PourSocialScore_GUI`).
- **Events:** `M.Events` (core bus). UIs listen (for example `OPTION_CHANGED`) and write through core setters.
- **Loader** (`PSS_Loader.lua`): `M.PSS_PARTS`; `NEEDS = {GUI="Libraries"}`; `M.PSS_Need(key)` loads a part and what it needs; a part whose version differs from core's prints a warning. Logging: `M.PSS_LoadLogging()`.
- **Options:** `M.PSS_OPTIONS` (one table; the window's Options tab renders from it); read `M.PSS_Opt`, write `M.PSS_SetOpt`; only non-defaults stored.
- **Helpers and kit:** `M.ShowMsg`, `M.Trim`; the window's kit (`ns.NewButton`, `ns.NewCheck`, `ns.NewEditBox`, `ns.NewRows`, ...; CLAUDE.md Interface, 09 code map).
- **Client flavour (B1T2):** `PSS_Flavour.lua` (core, after `PSS_Globals.lua`) builds `M.PSS_FLV` once at load (project id first, interface second), sets `V.wowIsRetail`, and offers `M.PSS_IsForever` (`M.PSS_IsCamelot` is the same function), `M.PSS_Can`, `M.PSS_CapLevel`. It replaces detector 1 (`V.wowIsRetail` in `PSS_Globals.lua`) and detector 2 (`M.PSS_IsCamelot` in `PSS_Options.lua`), both removed; `WHCAM` reads `M.PSS_IsForever()`; M.PSS_FLV.const per client (guildBankSlots, maxBagSize, lastBagIndex, battlePetCage); PSS_GwSyndicator.lua reads IsForever and IsRetail from the flavour record (Retail: IsForever false, IsRetail true); PSS_AddonsQuery.lua takes its addon-set digit from M.PSS_FLV (SAV-00092).
- **Capability reads (B1T3, B1T4):** the Forever-only reload branch (`PSS_OptionsView.lua`), macros, macro data and graphics (`PSS_Macros.lua`, `PSS_MacroData.lua`, `PSS_Graphics.lua`), surnames (`PSS_ChatBlock.lua`, off on Retail) and the compartment entry (`PSS_BlizzardUI.lua`) read `M.PSS_Can` / `M.PSS_IsForever`; no file tests the client itself.
- **Compat adapters (B3T1):** `PourSocialScore/PSS_Compat.lua` (core, after `PSS_Flavour.lua`, new) chooses each implementation once at load from `M.PSS_FLV.caps`: `M.PSS_CompatMenu(tag, fn)` (cap `modernMenu`), `M.PSS_CompatCompartment(entry)` (cap `compartment`), `M.PSS_CompatTemplate(kind)` (first template the client knows, cached per kind) and `M.PSS_ReloadMode()` (`"press"` when cap `reloadButton`, else `"direct"`), moved here from `PSS_OptionsView.lua`, whose `M.PSS_ReloadUI` and `M.PSS_ReloadPrompt` still call it. B3T10 adds `M.PSS_CompatFriendsAnchor(host)` (cap `friendsAnchor`): one reused `{ x = 2, y = 0, strata }` table for `FriendsFrame` on TBC Anniversary, else nil; `PourSocialScore_GUI/PSS_Window.lua` `M.PSS_WindowDock` anchors and sets the strata from it, and the open-from-command path restores `win.dockStrata` after `RestorePlace()`.
- **Compat bus services (B3T3):** `PourSocialScore_Libraries/PSS_CompatBus.lua` (new, after `PSS_Bus.lua`; the engine-rework re-cut point) declares `compat.containers.<size|read|invId|use|pickup>` and `compat.guildBank.<tabs|tab|read|money>` with `M.PSS_ProvideStub` (`onError = "empty"`, `writes = false`); the path is chosen once at load from `M.PSS_FLV.caps` (`cContainer`, `guildBank`); the key (bag or tab) is the input, `slot` and `out` go in opts, and `read` fills the reused `opts.out` set without allocating.
- **Compat bus call sites (B3T4):** `PourSocialScore_Libraries/PSS_ItemRead.lua` (bag reads, `item.slots`, equipped bag id, guild bank tab), `PSS_DttScan.lua` (one `compat.containers.read` per bag into a reused `out`) and `PSS_DttAct.lua` (`read`, `use`, `pickup` of the one bag) call `compat.containers` and `compat.guildBank` through `M.PSS_Call`, one call per bag or tab, never per slot; a non-viewable tab answers `false`.
- **Compat bus call sites (B3T5):** `PourSocialScore_Libraries/PSS_WhMeta.lua` (guild tab names via `compat.guildBank.tabs` and `.tab`; bag ids via `compat.containers.invId`), `PSS_WhContainers.lua` (guild row `has` = `M.PSS_Can("guildBank")`, `tabs` via `compat.guildBank.tabs`), `PSS_Money.lua` (guild bank gold via `compat.guildBank.money`); answers are guarded and copied out at once.
- **Counting and history:** `History.*` (04); totals `M.PSS_ListTotals` (Libraries).
- **Guild rules:** `M.PSS_GuildRuleActive`, `M.PSS_IsOwnGuild`; a rule's member count from `M.PSS_GuildMemberCount`.
- **Accessors (02 section 1.5):** guild member fields through `M.PSS_MemberCanon`, `M.PSS_MemberName`, `M.PSS_MemberGuildName`, `M.PSS_MemberWhenBlocked`, `M.PSS_MemberShipped`, `M.PSS_MemberPlayerKey` (one block in `PSS_GuildIgnoreList.lua`; `check_fields.lua`); counts totals through `History.CountTotal`; player list entries through `M.PSS_List`, `M.PSS_EntryName`, `M.PSS_EntryKind`, `M.PSS_NewEntry` and the `M.PSS_SetEntry*` setters (`PSS_PlayerIgnoreList.lua`).
- **Upgrades and schema:** `M.PSS_UPGRADE_REGISTRY`, `M.PSS_UPGRADE_RETIRED`, `M.PSS_RunUpgradeFields` / `M.PSS_RunUpgradeData`, `M.PSS_VersionOrder`; `M.PSS_SCHEMA`, `M.PSS_SchemaKnows`, `M.PSS_UnusableRules`.
- **GC helper:** `PSS_Perf.lua` (stepped collection; option `gcEnabled`). Event-driven only (P2): `M.PSS_RequestGC` runs out of combat and outside instances, waiting on events, never on a timer (02 section 4).
- **Commands:** `/pss` on its own opens or closes the window, and `/pss gui`, `ui` and `eui` are the same command (one alias table, `OPEN` in `PSS_Commands.lua`; the author, 2026-10-08); every other word is its own command and `/pss help` (or any word it does not know) lists them; `/pss export unused`; Camelot only: `/pss gfx`, `/pss macro` (cap reads gfxCommand and macroCommand of PSS_Flavour.lua; the Options reload button reads reloadButton) (Retail prints that they are Camelot only). On Camelot `/pss add`, `remove` and `expire` take two words as the name.
- **Window (09):** looks `M.PSS_UISkin()` (option `uiSkin`), accent `M.PSS_UIAccent()` (class colour), font `M.PSS_UIFontPath()` / `M.PSS_UIFontChoices()` (option `uiFont`), place `M.PSS_UIPlace` / `M.PSS_SetUIPlace` (Libraries `PSS_ViewNav.lua`); window `PSS_Window`, entry `M.PSS_OpenUI` (core) -> `M.PSS_OpenWindow`.

## 5. Crucial context
- Three TOCs per folder; one combined TOC broke Camelot.
- **Measured hot spots:** `PSS_GetGuildBlockCounts`, the shipped member walk and `PSS_GetMemberBlockCounts` make about 200 to 350 KB of garbage per full pass on the author's save. An estimate of 6 to 8 KB once measured 22 KB.
- **Known gaps:** downgrades lose data (issue #147); other open items in 06.
- **Known breaches of the baseline:** none (02 section 12, which also lists the approved overrides).
